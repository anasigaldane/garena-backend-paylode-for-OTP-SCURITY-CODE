Parental Guidance

Keeping children safe while gaming

We are committed to providing a safe and enjoyable gaming environment for players of all ages. This page provides information and resources for parents and guardians to help ensure a positive experience.

Garena Free Fire is rated for teens and above. We encourage parents to review the game content and features to determine if it is appropriate for their child.

In-Game Purchases

Managing spending and transactions

Our games may include options for in-game purchases. We strongly recommend that parents set up password protection for purchases on the devices their children use to prevent unauthorized transactions.

On most platforms, you can require a password for every purchase or set up parental controls to restrict spending.

Playtime Management

Balancing gaming with daily life

We encourage parents to monitor their children's playtime and ensure a healthy balance between gaming, school, physical activity, and social interactions. Consider setting daily time limits for gaming sessions.

Many devices and platforms offer built-in screen time management tools that can help enforce these limits.

Online Safety Tips

Best practices for young players

  • Remind your children not to share personal information with strangers online
  • Encourage them to report any inappropriate behavior or content immediately
  • Review their friend lists and privacy settings regularly
  • Discuss the importance of respectful communication with other players
  • Ensure they understand that not everyone online is who they claim to be

Account Controls

Securing your child's account

Parents can help manage their child's account by ensuring strong, unique passwords are used and enabling two-factor authentication for additional security. Regularly review account activity and linked devices.
